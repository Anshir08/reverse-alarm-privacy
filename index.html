Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Reverse Alarm is a local alarm clock designed to help you wake up by requiring you to complete challenges before an alarm can be dismissed. This Privacy Policy explains what information Reverse Alarm handles and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

Reverse Alarm does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or phone number inside the app.

Depending on the features you use, certain information may be processed by third-party services to provide specific app functionality.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Reverse Alarm stores core app information locally on your device, including:

  • Alarm schedules and alarm labels
  • Imported custom alarm sounds
  • Challenge settings
  • Wake-up progress, including streaks, XP, and completion history

This information remains on your device unless you choose to export it or use the Reset All Data feature.

3. Photos and Google Gemini

If you use a Medium or Hard photo challenge, Reverse Alarm uses a reduced version of the photo you provide to check whether the requested object appears in the image.

The image is sent to Google Gemini for this purpose. Reverse Alarm does not keep the photo in its own cloud storage.

Photo processing: A compressed JPEG and the requested object information may be sent to Google Gemini when you use a photo challenge.

4. Voice Recognition

If you use the voice challenge, your speech is processed by the operating system's speech recognition service.

Reverse Alarm does not store audio recordings. A short transcript may remain in memory while the challenge is active and is discarded when the challenge ends.

5. Advertising

Free users may see advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your region, Google may display a consent form before advertising is requested.

Advertising may use device advertising identifiers and app-activity signals to provide and measure advertising. Premium users do not see advertisements.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Paid plans and subscriptions are processed through Google Play and RevenueCat.

These services may receive purchase and entitlement information needed to process and verify your subscription. Reverse Alarm does not require an in-app account and does not collect your email address as part of the subscription process.

7. How We Share Information

Reverse Alarm does not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party service providers when necessary to provide features you explicitly use, including:

  • Google Gemini — photo challenge processing
  • Operating system speech recognition — voice challenge processing
  • Google AdMob — advertising for eligible free users
  • Google Play and RevenueCat — subscription and purchase processing

8. Information Security

Information transmitted to the third-party services described in this policy is sent using encrypted connections such as HTTPS where supported by those services.

Reverse Alarm does not maintain a user account database containing your name, email address, or phone number.

9. Data Deletion

You can use Reset All Data in the Reverse Alarm Settings screen to delete locally stored app data, including alarm schedules, preferences, progress, notifications, and custom sounds.

Reset All Data does not delete information that may already have been processed or retained by independent third-party services such as Google, AdMob, or RevenueCat. Requests concerning data held by those services may need to be made directly to the applicable provider.

You may also contact us if you have a question about deletion of data controlled by Reverse Alarm.

10. Children's Privacy

Reverse Alarm is not designed specifically for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Reverse Alarm's features, services, or data practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of data controlled by Reverse Alarm, contact us at:

support@reversealarm.app
