Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Reverse Alarm is a local alarm clock. We do not create accounts or collect your name, email, or phone number inside the app. On this device we store alarm times, labels, sounds you import, challenge settings, and wake-up progress (streak, XP, completion history). That data stays on the device unless you export it or use Reset All Data. If you use a Medium or Hard photo challenge, a reduced JPEG of the photo is sent to Google Gemini so we can check that the requested object is in the picture. We do not keep that photo in our cloud. If you use the voice challenge, the operating system’s speech recognizer processes your speech. We keep only a short transcript in memory until the challenge ends. Free users may see Google ads (AdMob). In regions that require it, we show Google’s consent form first. Ads use device advertising identifiers and app-activity signals. Premium hides ads. Paid plans are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. They receive purchase and entitlement data, not your alarm list. Contact support@reversealarm.app to ask questions or request deletion of data we control. Use Reset All Data in Settings to erase local data. You can also reset ad personalization in your Google account settings.
